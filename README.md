# Sri Lanka | Cost of Living Dashboard

An interactive Streamlit dashboard exploring Sri Lanka's cost of living through inflation, food prices, fuel prices, exchange rates, and the 2022 economic crisis.

## Dashboard Features

- **Economic Overview:** Inflation, fuel prices, and exchange-rate trends (2019–2025).
- **Food Price Analysis:** Historical changes in essential food prices.
- **Grocery Basket Explorer:** Cost of 12 essential commodities (2020–2026).
- **Price Shock Story:** Visual analysis of the 2022 economic crisis and its impact on grocery costs.
- **2027 Forecast Explorer:** Experimental grocery-cost forecasts with statistical model comparisons.

## Data Sources

- Department of Census and Statistics (DCS), Sri Lanka
- Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL)
- Macro Lab Sri Lanka — DCS weekly retail-price data

## Technologies

Python | Streamlit | Pandas | Plotly | Time-Series Forecasting

## Methodology Note

Food-price analysis uses Colombo retail prices. The grocery basket represents fixed illustrative quantities rather than official household expenditure weights. Forecasts are experimental and should not be interpreted as guaranteed future prices.
